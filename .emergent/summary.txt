The Dhruv AI project is an Indian B2C EdTech platform for competitive exams. It offers a personalized, hallucination-free Neuro-Symbolic AI mentor, featuring Personalized Learning Paths, Interactive Problem Solving, 24/7 Doubt Resolution, Advanced Performance Analytics, Realistic Mock Tests, and Stress Management/Motivational Modules.

**Mock Test Module:** Students generate AI-verified tests (Full-Length/Chapter-wise) with a dual-layer AI (Mentor for coaching, Professor for verification). It needs three generation modes (Standard, Variant, Adaptive), caching, database storage for tests, attempts, blueprints, and question deduplication. Retake options (Same Test, New Topics, Practice Weak Areas) and post-test review (Mentor feedback, Professor solutions, bookmarking, flashcards) are crucial. UI/UX includes subject-colored cards, progress bars, and quick actions.

**Auto-Note Mentor Module:** Transforms learning inputs (audio/video, slides, PDFs, images) into verified, structured, interactive study packs. Features include real-time transcription (tuned for Indian accents), OCR, NLP for content chunking, Professor-layer verification, Mentor-layer summarization, and spaced-repetition. Outputs are structured notes with interactive features. Frontend needs a Record Class button, live captions, progress bars, and searchable notes. Backend requires microservice architecture, asynchronous processing, MongoDB storage, caching, and a vector store. Privacy and performance are key.
