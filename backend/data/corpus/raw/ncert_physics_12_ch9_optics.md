# Ray Optics and Optical Instruments

## Reflection of Light

**Laws of Reflection:**
1. Incident ray, reflected ray, and normal lie in same plane
2. Angle of incidence = Angle of reflection (i = r)

**Plane Mirror:**
- Image is virtual, erect, same size
- Image distance = Object distance
- Lateral inversion occurs

**Spherical Mirrors:**

**Mirror Formula:**
1/v + 1/u = 1/f = 2/R

**Magnification:**
m = -v/u = h'/h

**Sign Convention (New Cartesian):**
- Distances measured from pole
- Along incident light: positive
- Against incident light: negative
- Above principal axis: positive
- Below principal axis: negative

## Refraction of Light

**Laws of Refraction:**
1. Incident ray, refracted ray, normal lie in same plane
2. Snell's Law: n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r

**Refractive Index:**
n = c/v = (speed in vacuum)/(speed in medium)

**Relative Refractive Index:**
n₂₁ = n₂/n₁ = v₁/v₂ = λ₁/λ₂

## Total Internal Reflection

Occurs when light goes from denser to rarer medium at angle > critical angle.

**Critical Angle:**
sin C = n₂/n₁ (where n₂ < n₁)

**Conditions:**
1. Light must travel from denser to rarer medium
2. Angle of incidence > Critical angle

**Applications:**
- Optical fibers
- Prisms in binoculars
- Diamond brilliance
- Mirages

## Refraction at Spherical Surfaces

**Formula:**
n₂/v - n₁/u = (n₂ - n₁)/R

## Thin Lens Formula

**Lens Maker's Equation:**
1/f = (n - 1)(1/R₁ - 1/R₂)

**Thin Lens Formula:**
1/v - 1/u = 1/f

**Magnification:**
m = v/u = h'/h

**Power of Lens:**
P = 1/f (in meters)

Units: Diopter (D)

**Combination of Lenses (in contact):**
1/f = 1/f₁ + 1/f₂ + ...
P = P₁ + P₂ + ...

## Prism

**Deviation:**
δ = i + e - A

where A = angle of prism

**Minimum Deviation:**
At minimum deviation: i = e, r₁ = r₂ = A/2

**Formula for n:**
n = sin[(A + δₘ)/2] / sin(A/2)

**Dispersion:**
Different wavelengths refract differently
n_violet > n_red
δ_violet > δ_red

**Angular Dispersion:**
θ = δ_v - δ_r = (n_v - n_r)A

**Dispersive Power:**
ω = (n_v - n_r)/(n_y - 1)

## Optical Instruments

**Simple Microscope:**
Magnifying power: M = 1 + D/f (for image at D)
M = D/f (for image at infinity)

where D = 25 cm (least distance of distinct vision)

**Compound Microscope:**
M = m_o × m_e = (L/f_o) × (D/f_e)

**Telescope (Astronomical):**
M = f_o/f_e (for image at infinity)
Length = f_o + f_e

**Resolving Power:**

Microscope: RP = 2n sin θ/λ

Telescope: RP = D/1.22λ
