# Gravitation

## Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation

**Statement:**
Every particle attracts every other particle with a force proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.

**Formula:**
F = G(m₁m₂)/r²

where:
- G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg² (Universal Gravitational Constant)
- m₁, m₂ = masses
- r = distance between centers

**Vector Form:**
F₁₂ = -G(m₁m₂/r²)r̂₁₂

(Negative sign indicates attractive force)

## Acceleration Due to Gravity

**At Earth's Surface:**
g = GM/R²

where:
- M = mass of Earth (5.97 × 10²⁴ kg)
- R = radius of Earth (6.37 × 10⁶ m)
- g ≈ 9.8 m/s²

**Variation with Height:**
g_h = g(R/(R+h))² ≈ g(1 - 2h/R) for h << R

**Variation with Depth:**
g_d = g(1 - d/R)

At center (d = R): g = 0

**Variation with Latitude:**
g_φ = g - Rω²cos²φ

At poles (φ = 90°): g maximum
At equator (φ = 0°): g minimum

## Gravitational Potential Energy

**Definition:**
Work done to bring mass from infinity to point.

**Formula:**
U = -GMm/r

**Key Points:**
- Always negative (bound system)
- Zero at infinity
- U increases (becomes less negative) as r increases

**Near Earth's Surface:**
U = mgh (with surface as reference)

## Gravitational Potential

**Definition:**
Potential energy per unit mass.

**Formula:**
V = -GM/r

**Units:** J/kg or m²/s²

**Relation:**
g = -dV/dr

## Escape Velocity

**Definition:**
Minimum velocity to escape Earth's gravitational field.

**Formula:**
v_e = √(2GM/R) = √(2gR)

For Earth: v_e ≈ 11.2 km/s

**Derivation:**
KE at surface = PE gained to reach infinity
½mv_e² = GMm/R

## Orbital Velocity

**For circular orbit at height h:**
v_o = √(GM/(R+h)) = √(gR²/(R+h))

**Near surface:** v_o = √(gR) ≈ 7.9 km/s

**Relation with escape velocity:**
v_e = √2 × v_o

## Satellite Motion

**Time Period:**
T = 2π√((R+h)³/GM) = 2π√((R+h)³/(gR²))

**Kepler's Third Law:**
T² ∝ r³

**Energy of Satellite:**
- KE = GMm/2r = ½mv_o²
- PE = -GMm/r
- Total E = -GMm/2r = -KE

**Geostationary Satellite:**
- Period = 24 hours
- Height ≈ 36,000 km
- Above equator, moves with Earth
- Used for communication

## Kepler's Laws of Planetary Motion

**First Law (Law of Orbits):**
Planets move in elliptical orbits with Sun at one focus.

**Second Law (Law of Areas):**
Line joining planet and Sun sweeps equal areas in equal times.
(Conservation of angular momentum)

**Third Law (Law of Periods):**
T² ∝ a³

where a = semi-major axis of ellipse

T²/a³ = 4π²/(GM)

## Weightlessness

**Apparent Weight:**
W_apparent = m(g - a)

**True Weightlessness:**
- At center of Earth (g = 0)
- In deep space (no gravitational field)

**Apparent Weightlessness:**
- In free fall (a = g)
- In orbiting satellite (centripetal acceleration = g)
- At neutral point between Earth and Moon

## Binding Energy

**Definition:**
Energy required to remove satellite from orbit to infinity.

**Formula:**
BE = -E = GMm/2r = ½mv_o²

**To escape from orbit:**
Need additional energy = GMm/2r
