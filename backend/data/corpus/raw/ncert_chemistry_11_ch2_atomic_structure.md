# Structure of Atom

## Atomic Models

**Thomson's Model (Plum Pudding):**
- Atom is sphere of positive charge
- Electrons embedded like plums in pudding
- Failed to explain scattering experiments

**Rutherford's Nuclear Model:**
- Most mass and positive charge in small nucleus
- Electrons revolve around nucleus
- Most of atom is empty space
- Failed to explain stability and line spectra

**Bohr's Model:**
- Electrons in fixed circular orbits
- Energy quantized: E = -13.6/n² eV
- Radius of orbit: r_n = 0.529 × n² Å
- Explained hydrogen spectrum

## Quantum Numbers

**Principal Quantum Number (n):**
- Shell number: 1, 2, 3, 4... (K, L, M, N...)
- Energy level
- Maximum electrons in shell = 2n²

**Azimuthal Quantum Number (l):**
- Subshell: 0, 1, 2, 3... (s, p, d, f...)
- l = 0 to (n-1)
- Shape of orbital
- Orbital angular momentum = √[l(l+1)]ℏ

**Magnetic Quantum Number (m_l):**
- Orientation of orbital
- m_l = -l to +l
- Number of orbitals = 2l + 1

**Spin Quantum Number (m_s):**
- Spin of electron: +½ or -½
- Two electrons per orbital with opposite spins

## Electronic Configuration

**Aufbau Principle:**
Electrons fill orbitals in order of increasing energy.
Order: 1s, 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, 4s, 3d, 4p, 5s, 4d, 5p, 6s, 4f...

**Pauli Exclusion Principle:**
No two electrons can have same set of all four quantum numbers.
Maximum 2 electrons per orbital.

**Hund's Rule:**
Electrons occupy degenerate orbitals singly first with parallel spins.

**Examples:**
- C (6): 1s² 2s² 2p²
- O (8): 1s² 2s² 2p⁴
- Fe (26): 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s² 3p⁶ 4s² 3d⁶
- Exceptions: Cr (24): [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹, Cu (29): [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s¹

## Atomic Spectra

**Emission Spectrum:**
Atoms emit light at specific wavelengths when excited

**Hydrogen Spectrum Series:**

| Series | Transition | Region |
|--------|------------|--------|
| Lyman | n → 1 | UV |
| Balmer | n → 2 | Visible |
| Paschen | n → 3 | IR |
| Brackett | n → 4 | IR |
| Pfund | n → 5 | Far IR |

**Rydberg Formula:**
1/λ = R_H(1/n₁² - 1/n₂²)

R_H = 1.097 × 10⁷ m⁻¹

## Dual Nature of Matter

**de Broglie Hypothesis:**
All matter has wave nature.

**de Broglie Wavelength:**
λ = h/p = h/(mv)

**Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle:**
Cannot simultaneously know exact position and momentum.

Δx × Δp ≥ h/(4π)
Δx × Δv ≥ h/(4πm)

## Orbitals and Shapes

**s orbital:** Spherical, l = 0
**p orbital:** Dumbbell shape, l = 1, 3 orientations (px, py, pz)
**d orbital:** Double dumbbell/cloverleaf, l = 2, 5 orientations
**f orbital:** Complex shapes, l = 3, 7 orientations

**Nodes:**
- Radial nodes = n - l - 1
- Angular nodes = l
- Total nodes = n - 1
