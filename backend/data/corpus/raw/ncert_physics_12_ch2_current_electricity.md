# Current Electricity

## Electric Current

Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge.

**Formula:**
I = Q/t = dQ/dt

**Units:** Ampere (A) = Coulomb/second

**Direction:** Conventional current flows from positive to negative (opposite to electron flow)

## Drift Velocity and Current

**Drift Velocity:** Average velocity of electrons in a conductor due to applied field.

**Formula:**
vd = eEτ/m = I/(neA)

where:
- e = electron charge (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)
- E = electric field
- τ = relaxation time
- m = electron mass
- n = number density of electrons
- A = cross-sectional area

**Current Density:**
J = I/A = nevd = σE

## Ohm's Law

The current through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across it, at constant temperature.

**Formula:**
V = IR

where:
- V = potential difference (Volts)
- I = current (Amperes)
- R = resistance (Ohms)

**Ohmic Conductors:** Follow Ohm's law (metals at constant temperature)
**Non-ohmic Conductors:** Don't follow (diodes, transistors)

## Resistance and Resistivity

**Resistance:**
R = ρL/A

where:
- ρ = resistivity (Ω·m)
- L = length
- A = cross-sectional area

**Conductivity:** σ = 1/ρ

**Temperature Dependence (metals):**
R_T = R₀(1 + αT)
ρ_T = ρ₀(1 + αT)

where α = temperature coefficient of resistance

## Combination of Resistors

**Series Connection:**
- Same current through all
- R_eq = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ + ...
- V_total = V₁ + V₂ + V₃ + ...

**Parallel Connection:**
- Same voltage across all
- 1/R_eq = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃ + ...
- I_total = I₁ + I₂ + I₃ + ...

**For two resistors in parallel:**
R_eq = (R₁ × R₂)/(R₁ + R₂)

## EMF and Internal Resistance

**EMF (ε):** Work done by cell per unit charge in complete circuit

**Terminal Voltage:**
V = ε - Ir

where r = internal resistance of cell

**In circuit:**
I = ε/(R + r)

**Maximum Power Transfer:**
Occurs when R = r (external = internal resistance)

## Kirchhoff's Laws

**First Law (Junction Rule):**
Sum of currents entering a junction = Sum of currents leaving
∑I_in = ∑I_out

(Conservation of charge)

**Second Law (Loop Rule):**
Sum of potential differences around any closed loop = 0
∑V = 0

(Conservation of energy)

**Sign Convention:**
- EMF: + if loop goes from - to + terminal
- Resistor: + if loop goes against current direction

## Electrical Power and Energy

**Power:**
P = VI = I²R = V²/R

**Units:** Watt (W) = Joule/second

**Energy:**
E = Pt = VIt = I²Rt = V²t/R

**Units:** Joule (J), kilowatt-hour (kWh)
1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J

## Wheatstone Bridge

Balanced when no current flows through galvanometer.

**Balance Condition:**
P/Q = R/S

or equivalently: PS = QR

**Applications:**
- Measurement of unknown resistance
- Meter bridge (slide wire bridge)
- Post office box
