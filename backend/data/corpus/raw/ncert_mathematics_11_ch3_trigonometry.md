# Trigonometric Functions

## Angles and Measurement

**Degree Measure:**
1 complete rotation = 360°
1° = 60 minutes (60')
1' = 60 seconds (60")

**Radian Measure:**
1 radian = angle at center subtending arc equal to radius
π radians = 180°

**Conversion:**
- Degrees to radians: θ(rad) = θ(deg) × π/180
- Radians to degrees: θ(deg) = θ(rad) × 180/π

**Arc Length:**
l = rθ (θ in radians)

**Area of Sector:**
A = ½r²θ

## Trigonometric Ratios

**In right triangle:**
- sin θ = Opposite/Hypotenuse
- cos θ = Adjacent/Hypotenuse
- tan θ = Opposite/Adjacent = sin θ/cos θ
- cot θ = Adjacent/Opposite = cos θ/sin θ = 1/tan θ
- sec θ = Hypotenuse/Adjacent = 1/cos θ
- cosec θ = Hypotenuse/Opposite = 1/sin θ

## Standard Values

| θ | 0° | 30° | 45° | 60° | 90° |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sin θ | 0 | 1/2 | 1/√2 | √3/2 | 1 |
| cos θ | 1 | √3/2 | 1/√2 | 1/2 | 0 |
| tan θ | 0 | 1/√3 | 1 | √3 | ∞ |

## Fundamental Identities

**Pythagorean Identities:**
- sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
- 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ
- 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ

**Reciprocal Relations:**
- sin θ × cosec θ = 1
- cos θ × sec θ = 1
- tan θ × cot θ = 1

## Signs in Quadrants

**ASTC Rule (All Students Take Coffee):**
- Q1: All positive
- Q2: Sin, Cosec positive
- Q3: Tan, Cot positive
- Q4: Cos, Sec positive

## Allied Angles

**Formulas for (90° ± θ):**
- sin(90° - θ) = cos θ
- cos(90° - θ) = sin θ
- sin(90° + θ) = cos θ
- cos(90° + θ) = -sin θ

**Formulas for (180° ± θ):**
- sin(180° - θ) = sin θ
- cos(180° - θ) = -cos θ
- sin(180° + θ) = -sin θ
- cos(180° + θ) = -cos θ

**Formulas for (360° - θ) = (-θ):**
- sin(-θ) = -sin θ (odd function)
- cos(-θ) = cos θ (even function)
- tan(-θ) = -tan θ (odd function)

## Compound Angle Formulas

**Sum Formulas:**
- sin(A + B) = sin A cos B + cos A sin B
- cos(A + B) = cos A cos B - sin A sin B
- tan(A + B) = (tan A + tan B)/(1 - tan A tan B)

**Difference Formulas:**
- sin(A - B) = sin A cos B - cos A sin B
- cos(A - B) = cos A cos B + sin A sin B
- tan(A - B) = (tan A - tan B)/(1 + tan A tan B)

## Multiple Angle Formulas

**Double Angle:**
- sin 2A = 2 sin A cos A
- cos 2A = cos²A - sin²A = 2cos²A - 1 = 1 - 2sin²A
- tan 2A = 2 tan A/(1 - tan²A)

**Triple Angle:**
- sin 3A = 3 sin A - 4 sin³A
- cos 3A = 4 cos³A - 3 cos A
- tan 3A = (3 tan A - tan³A)/(1 - 3 tan²A)

**Half Angle:**
- sin²(A/2) = (1 - cos A)/2
- cos²(A/2) = (1 + cos A)/2
- tan(A/2) = sin A/(1 + cos A) = (1 - cos A)/sin A

## Sum/Difference to Product

- sin C + sin D = 2 sin((C+D)/2) cos((C-D)/2)
- sin C - sin D = 2 cos((C+D)/2) sin((C-D)/2)
- cos C + cos D = 2 cos((C+D)/2) cos((C-D)/2)
- cos C - cos D = -2 sin((C+D)/2) sin((C-D)/2)

## Product to Sum

- 2 sin A cos B = sin(A+B) + sin(A-B)
- 2 cos A sin B = sin(A+B) - sin(A-B)
- 2 cos A cos B = cos(A+B) + cos(A-B)
- 2 sin A sin B = cos(A-B) - cos(A+B)

## Trigonometric Equations

**General Solutions:**
- sin θ = sin α ⟹ θ = nπ + (-1)ⁿα
- cos θ = cos α ⟹ θ = 2nπ ± α
- tan θ = tan α ⟹ θ = nπ + α

where n is any integer.
