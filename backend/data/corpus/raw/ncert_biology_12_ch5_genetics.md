# Principles of Inheritance and Variation

## Mendelian Genetics

**Mendel's Experiments:**
- Used garden pea (Pisum sativum)
- Studied 7 contrasting traits
- Self-pollination allowed pure lines

## Mendel's Laws

**1. Law of Dominance:**
- In heterozygote, one allele dominates
- Dominant allele expressed, recessive masked
- Example: Tt shows tall phenotype

**2. Law of Segregation:**
- Two alleles of a gene separate during gamete formation
- Each gamete receives one allele
- Also called "Law of Purity of Gametes"

**3. Law of Independent Assortment:**
- Alleles of different genes assort independently
- Valid for genes on different chromosomes
- Basis of 9:3:3:1 ratio in dihybrid cross

## Monohybrid Cross

**Cross:** TT × tt

**F1 generation:** All Tt (tall)

**F2 generation (F1 × F1):**
- Genotype ratio: 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt
- Phenotype ratio: 3 tall : 1 short

**Test Cross:**
- Cross with homozygous recessive
- To determine genotype of dominant phenotype
- Tt × tt → 1 Tt : 1 tt (1:1 ratio indicates heterozygous)

## Dihybrid Cross

**Cross:** RRYY × rryy (Round Yellow × wrinkled green)

**F1:** All RrYy (Round Yellow)

**F2 Phenotypic Ratio:** 9:3:3:1
- 9 Round Yellow
- 3 Round green
- 3 wrinkled Yellow
- 1 wrinkled green

**F2 Genotypic Ratio:** 1:2:1:2:4:2:1:2:1

## Incomplete Dominance

**Neither allele completely dominant.**

**Example:** Flower color in snapdragon
- RR: Red
- rr: White
- Rr: Pink

**F2 ratio:** 1 Red : 2 Pink : 1 White (1:2:1)

## Co-dominance

**Both alleles expressed equally.**

**Example:** ABO blood group
- Iᴬ and Iᴮ are co-dominant
- i is recessive
- Iᴬi = A, Iᴮi = B, IᴬIᴮ = AB, ii = O

## Multiple Alleles

**More than 2 alleles exist in population.**

**Example:** ABO blood groups
- 3 alleles: Iᴬ, Iᴮ, i
- 6 genotypes, 4 phenotypes

## Polygenic Inheritance

**Multiple genes control one trait.**

**Example:** Skin color, height, intelligence
- Continuous variation
- Additive effects
- Bell-shaped distribution

## Pleiotropy

**One gene affects multiple traits.**

**Example:** Sickle cell anemia
- Single mutation in β-globin gene
- Affects RBC shape, oxygen carrying, multiple organs

## Sex Determination

**XX-XY System (Humans):**
- Females: XX (homogametic)
- Males: XY (heterogametic)
- Y chromosome determines maleness (SRY gene)

**Other Systems:**
- ZW-ZZ: Birds, some insects (female = ZW)
- X0: Grasshoppers (male = X0)
- Haplodiploidy: Bees (males = haploid)

## Sex-Linked Inheritance

**X-linked traits:**
- Genes on X chromosome
- More commonly expressed in males (only one X)
- Examples: Hemophilia, color blindness

**Inheritance Pattern (Color blindness):**
- Carrier female (XᴮXᵇ) × Normal male (XᴮY)
- Daughters: 50% carrier, 50% normal
- Sons: 50% affected, 50% normal

## Pedigree Analysis

**Symbols:**
- Circle: Female
- Square: Male
- Filled: Affected
- Half-filled: Carrier
- Horizontal line: Mating
- Vertical line: Offspring

**Patterns:**
- Autosomal dominant: Appears every generation
- Autosomal recessive: Skips generations, consanguinity common
- X-linked recessive: More males affected, no father-to-son

## Chromosomal Disorders

**Aneuploidy:**
- Monosomy: 2n - 1 (Turner's: 45, X0)
- Trisomy: 2n + 1 (Down's: 47, +21)

**Examples:**
- Down syndrome: Trisomy 21
- Turner syndrome: 45, X0 (female)
- Klinefelter syndrome: 47, XXY (male)
