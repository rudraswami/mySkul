# Motion in a Plane

## Scalar and Vector Quantities

**Scalars:** Have magnitude only (mass, time, speed, distance, temperature)

**Vectors:** Have magnitude and direction (displacement, velocity, acceleration, force)

**Vector Notation:**
- Bold letter: **A** or arrow notation: Ā
- Magnitude: |A| or A

## Vector Addition and Subtraction

**Triangle Law:** Place vectors head-to-tail, resultant joins tail of first to head of last.

**Parallelogram Law:** Two vectors from same point form parallelogram, diagonal is resultant.

**Resultant of Two Vectors:**
R = √(A² + B² + 2AB cos θ)

Direction: tan α = (B sin θ)/(A + B cos θ)

**Special Cases:**
- θ = 0° (same direction): R = A + B
- θ = 180° (opposite): R = |A - B|
- θ = 90° (perpendicular): R = √(A² + B²)

## Resolution of Vectors

Any vector can be resolved into components:

**In 2D:**
- Aₓ = A cos θ (horizontal component)
- Aᵧ = A sin θ (vertical component)
- A = √(Aₓ² + Aᵧ²)
- θ = tan⁻¹(Aᵧ/Aₓ)

**Unit Vectors:**
- î (along x-axis)
- ĵ (along y-axis)
- k̂ (along z-axis)

A = Aₓî + Aᵧĵ + Aᵤk̂

## Projectile Motion

Motion of object thrown at an angle to horizontal under gravity.

**Key Assumptions:**
- Air resistance negligible
- g is constant and downward
- Earth's curvature ignored

**Equations (θ = angle of projection, u = initial speed):**

**Time of Flight:**
T = 2u sin θ / g

**Maximum Height:**
H = u² sin² θ / 2g

**Horizontal Range:**
R = u² sin 2θ / g

**Maximum Range:** At θ = 45°, R_max = u²/g

**Key Points:**
- Horizontal velocity remains constant: uₓ = u cos θ
- Vertical velocity changes: vᵧ = u sin θ - gt
- Trajectory is parabolic
- Range is same for θ and (90° - θ)

## Uniform Circular Motion

Motion in a circle at constant speed.

**Key Facts:**
- Speed is constant but velocity changes direction
- Acceleration is centripetal (toward center)
- Velocity is always tangent to circle

**Centripetal Acceleration:**
aᶜ = v²/r = ω²r

**Centripetal Force:**
Fᶜ = mv²/r = mω²r

**Angular Quantities:**
- Angular velocity: ω = 2π/T = 2πf (rad/s)
- Time period: T = 2πr/v
- Frequency: f = 1/T

**Common Mistakes:**
1. Calling centripetal force as "centrifugal"
2. Forgetting that centripetal force is not a new force
3. Not recognizing circular motion in problems

## Relative Velocity in 2D

**Rain-Umbrella Problem:**
- Rain appears to come at angle
- v_rain_relative = v_rain - v_person
- Tilt umbrella in direction of relative velocity

**River Crossing:**
- To cross shortest path: swim at angle upstream
- To cross in minimum time: swim perpendicular to flow
- Drift = (velocity of river) × (time to cross)
