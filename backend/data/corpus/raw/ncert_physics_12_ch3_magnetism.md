# Magnetism and Magnetic Effects of Current

## Magnetic Field

Magnetic field is a region where a magnetic force is experienced by moving charges.

**Magnetic Field Lines:**
- Form closed loops (no magnetic monopoles)
- Direction: from North to South outside magnet
- Never intersect
- Closer lines = stronger field

**Units:** Tesla (T) = Wb/m² = N/(A·m)

## Biot-Savart Law

Magnetic field due to a current element.

**Formula:**
dB = (μ₀/4π) × (I dl × r̂)/r²

where:
- μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ T·m/A (permeability of free space)
- I = current
- dl = length element
- r = distance from element

**Field at Center of Circular Loop:**
B = μ₀I/2R

**Field on Axis of Circular Loop:**
B = μ₀IR²/[2(R² + x²)^(3/2)]

## Ampere's Circuital Law

**Statement:**
∮B·dl = μ₀I_enclosed

The line integral of B around a closed loop equals μ₀ times the current enclosed.

**Applications:**

**Infinite Straight Wire:**
B = μ₀I/(2πr)

**Solenoid:**
B = μ₀nI (inside)
B ≈ 0 (outside)

where n = N/L = number of turns per unit length

**Toroid:**
B = μ₀NI/(2πr) (inside)
B = 0 (outside)

## Force on Moving Charge (Lorentz Force)

**Magnetic Force:**
F = qv × B

Magnitude: F = qvB sin θ

**Direction:** Use right-hand rule

**Key Points:**
- Force is perpendicular to both v and B
- Force does no work (doesn't change KE)
- Charge moves in circular path if v ⊥ B

**Radius of circular path:**
r = mv/(qB)

**Time period:**
T = 2πm/(qB) (independent of velocity!)

## Force on Current-Carrying Conductor

**Formula:**
F = IL × B

Magnitude: F = BIL sin θ

**Direction:** Right-hand rule

## Force Between Parallel Currents

**Two parallel wires:**
F/L = μ₀I₁I₂/(2πd)

**Direction:**
- Same direction currents: Attract
- Opposite direction currents: Repel

**Definition of Ampere:**
1 A is the current which, when flowing through two parallel infinite wires 1 m apart, produces force of 2 × 10⁻⁷ N/m.

## Torque on Current Loop

A current loop in a magnetic field experiences a torque.

**Torque:**
τ = NBIA sin θ = M × B

where:
- N = number of turns
- B = magnetic field
- I = current
- A = area of loop
- M = NIA = magnetic moment

**Potential Energy:**
U = -M·B = -MB cos θ

**Stable equilibrium:** θ = 0 (M parallel to B)
**Unstable equilibrium:** θ = 180° (M antiparallel to B)

## Moving Coil Galvanometer

**Principle:** Torque on current loop in magnetic field

**Deflection:**
θ = (NAB/k) × I

where k = torsional constant of spring

**Current Sensitivity:** θ/I = NAB/k
**Voltage Sensitivity:** θ/V = NAB/(kR)

**Conversion to Ammeter:**
Add low resistance (shunt) S in parallel
S = Ig × G/(I - Ig)

**Conversion to Voltmeter:**
Add high resistance R in series
R = V/Ig - G
