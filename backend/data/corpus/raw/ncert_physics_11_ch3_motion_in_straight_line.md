# Motion in a Straight Line

## Distance and Displacement

**Distance** is the total path length traveled by an object. It is a scalar quantity.

**Displacement** is the change in position of an object. It is a vector quantity with both magnitude and direction.

**Key Differences:**
- Distance ≥ |Displacement| always
- Distance is always positive, displacement can be negative
- For straight line motion in one direction: Distance = |Displacement|

**Formula:**
Displacement Δx = x₂ - x₁

## Speed and Velocity

**Speed** is the rate of change of distance traveled. It is a scalar.
- Average speed = Total distance / Total time
- Instantaneous speed = |dx/dt|

**Velocity** is the rate of change of displacement. It is a vector.
- Average velocity = Displacement / Time = Δx/Δt
- Instantaneous velocity = dx/dt

**Key Points:**
- Speed is always positive
- Velocity can be positive or negative
- Average speed ≥ |Average velocity|

## Acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.

**Formulas:**
- Average acceleration: a_avg = Δv/Δt = (v - u)/t
- Instantaneous acceleration: a = dv/dt = d²x/dt²

**Sign Convention:**
- Positive acceleration: velocity increasing in positive direction
- Negative acceleration (deceleration): velocity decreasing

## Equations of Motion (SUVAT)

For uniformly accelerated motion (constant acceleration):

**First Equation (Velocity-Time):**
v = u + at

**Second Equation (Displacement-Time):**
s = ut + ½at²

**Third Equation (Velocity-Displacement):**
v² = u² + 2as

**Average Velocity Method:**
s = ((u + v)/2) × t

where:
- u = initial velocity (m/s)
- v = final velocity (m/s)
- a = acceleration (m/s²)
- t = time (s)
- s = displacement (m)

**Common Mistakes:**
1. Using these equations for non-uniform acceleration
2. Sign errors with direction
3. Confusing distance with displacement

## Free Fall Motion

Motion under gravity alone (air resistance negligible).

**Key Facts:**
- g = 9.8 m/s² (or 10 m/s² for approximation)
- g is positive downward (convention)
- All objects fall with same acceleration regardless of mass

**Equations for free fall:**
- v = u + gt (downward positive)
- h = ut + ½gt²
- v² = u² + 2gh

**For upward throw:**
- Use g = -9.8 m/s² (negative)
- Time to reach max height: t = u/g
- Max height: H = u²/2g
- Time of flight: T = 2u/g

## Relative Motion

**Relative Velocity:**
Velocity of A relative to B: v_AB = v_A - v_B

**Key Applications:**
1. Two objects moving in same direction: v_rel = |v₁ - v₂|
2. Two objects moving in opposite directions: v_rel = |v₁ + v₂|
3. Rain and umbrella problems
4. River crossing problems

## Graphical Analysis

**Displacement-Time (x-t) Graph:**
- Slope = velocity
- Straight line = constant velocity
- Curve = changing velocity
- Horizontal line = at rest

**Velocity-Time (v-t) Graph:**
- Slope = acceleration
- Area under curve = displacement
- Straight line = constant acceleration
- Horizontal line = constant velocity

**Acceleration-Time (a-t) Graph:**
- Area under curve = change in velocity
