# Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure

## Types of Chemical Bonds

**Ionic Bond:**
- Transfer of electrons
- Between metals and nonmetals
- High melting/boiling points
- Conduct electricity when molten

**Covalent Bond:**
- Sharing of electrons
- Between nonmetals
- Lower melting points
- Poor conductors

**Coordinate Bond:**
- Shared pair from one atom only
- Donor (Lewis base) → Acceptor (Lewis acid)
- Example: NH₄⁺, H₃O⁺

## Lewis Structures and Octet Rule

**Octet Rule:** Atoms tend to achieve 8 electrons in outer shell.

**Exceptions to Octet:**
1. Incomplete octet: BF₃, BeCl₂
2. Expanded octet: PCl₅, SF₆ (d orbitals)
3. Odd electron molecules: NO, NO₂

**Formal Charge:**
FC = Valence e⁻ - (Non-bonding e⁻ + ½ Bonding e⁻)

Best structure has minimum formal charges.

## VSEPR Theory

**Principle:** Electron pairs repel each other to maximize distance.

**Shapes based on electron pairs:**
| Total pairs | Bonding | Lone | Shape | Example |
|-------------|---------|------|-------|---------|
| 2 | 2 | 0 | Linear | BeCl₂ |
| 3 | 3 | 0 | Trigonal planar | BF₃ |
| 3 | 2 | 1 | Bent | SO₂ |
| 4 | 4 | 0 | Tetrahedral | CH₄ |
| 4 | 3 | 1 | Trigonal pyramidal | NH₃ |
| 4 | 2 | 2 | Bent | H₂O |
| 5 | 5 | 0 | Trigonal bipyramidal | PCl₅ |
| 6 | 6 | 0 | Octahedral | SF₆ |

**Bond Angles:**
- Lone pair-lone pair > Lone pair-bond pair > Bond pair-bond pair repulsion
- Lone pairs compress bond angles

## Hybridization

**Definition:** Mixing of atomic orbitals to form equivalent hybrid orbitals.

**Types:**
- sp: Linear (180°) - BeCl₂, C₂H₂
- sp²: Trigonal planar (120°) - BF₃, C₂H₄
- sp³: Tetrahedral (109.5°) - CH₄, NH₃, H₂O
- sp³d: Trigonal bipyramidal - PCl₅
- sp³d²: Octahedral - SF₆

**Formula:**
Hybridization = ½(V + M - C + A)
where V = valence electrons, M = monovalent atoms, C = cation charge, A = anion charge

## Molecular Orbital Theory

**Principles:**
1. Atomic orbitals combine to form molecular orbitals
2. Number of MOs = Number of AOs combined
3. Bonding MO (lower energy) and Antibonding MO (higher energy)

**Bond Order:**
BO = ½(Bonding electrons - Antibonding electrons)

**MO Energy Order:**
For O₂, F₂: σ1s < σ*1s < σ2s < σ*2s < σ2p < π2p < π*2p < σ*2p

For N₂, C₂: σ1s < σ*1s < σ2s < σ*2s < π2p < σ2p < π*2p < σ*2p

**Magnetic Properties:**
- Paramagnetic: Unpaired electrons (O₂)
- Diamagnetic: All paired electrons (N₂)

## Hydrogen Bonding

**Conditions:**
1. H bonded to highly electronegative atom (F, O, N)
2. Lone pair on another F, O, N atom

**Types:**
- Intermolecular: Between different molecules (H₂O)
- Intramolecular: Within same molecule (salicylaldehyde)

**Effects:**
- High boiling points
- Solubility in water
- Viscosity
- Anomalous properties of water

## Resonance

When single Lewis structure cannot explain properties.

**Resonance Structures:**
- Same atom positions
- Different electron arrangement
- Actual structure is hybrid

**Examples:**
- O₃ (two equivalent structures)
- CO₃²⁻ (three equivalent structures)
- Benzene (two Kekulé structures)
